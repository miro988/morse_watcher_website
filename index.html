Morse code app for iPhone

Read Morse code from any flashing light.

  1. Point your camera at the light.
  2. Tap the light to target it. The message appears as text.
  3. Reply with your flashlight.

Free 14-day trial. Works offline.

What it does

Decode

Morse from flashlights, lamps and other phones. It learns the sender's speed by itself.

Send

Any message with your iPhone flashlight.

SOS Free

Need rescue with no signal? Flash SOS, the international call for help, and make yourself easy to spot from a distance.

Alert Free

A rapid strobe that helps drivers see you better on dark roads and at crosswalks, for safer walks at night.
