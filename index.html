What it does

Decode

Point your camera at a light flashing Morse code, tap it, and Morse Watcher detects the pulses and translates them to text in real time.

Send

Type a message, hit send, and Morse Watcher flashes your torch in Morse code.

SOS Torch Free

Flash SOS, the international distress signal, to people who can see your light.

Driver Alert Free

A bright, fast-flashing light that helps catch drivers' attention when you're out in the dark.

Uses

When your phone has no signal

Get it before you need it. Without internet, you can't reach the App Store.

No bars, no Wi-Fi, no internet. Your flashlight and camera still work, and Morse Watcher turns them into a way to send messages: one phone flashes, the other reads it as text. It works when both phones have the app and can see each other's light, and works best when it's dark outside.

Emergencies SOS

Off-grid with no signal? Flash SOS, the international distress signal, to people who can see your light.

Internet shutdowns

Send short messages by light, within sight, when networks are cut or unavailable.

Hiking & camping

Send short messages between campsites or across open ground when you can see each other's light.

Landmarks & beacons

Read Morse from beacons and buildings that flash messages.

Night walks Alert

The free Alert strobe can help signal your presence to drivers on dark roads.

Learning Morse

Practice sending and check your reading against live decoding.

Scouts & games

Decode a blinking light at camp, in escape rooms or in night games.
