Best Morse Code Apps for iPhone (2026)

Short answer: to read a flashing light with your iPhone camera, use Morse Watcher. To decode Morse audio (radio CW, beeps) or learn Morse, use Morse-It. For a free light-chat app, try Morse Code: Offline Chat.

1. Morse Watcher: best for reading a flashing light

Morse Watcher is built for one job: point the camera at a flashing light (a flashlight, headlamp, beacon, ship lamp or another phone) and read the message as text in real time. You tap the light to target it. The app then learns the sender's dot and dash timing automatically, so it keeps up with slow, sloppy or fast senders without you setting a speed. It supports telephoto and optical zoom for distant lights, plays an audio tone while the light is on, and works completely offline. It can also send replies with the flashlight, and its SOS and alert strobe buttons are free.

Downsides: it doesn't decode audio, and it isn't a course for learning Morse. After the 14-day trial, decoding requires a subscription. Requires iOS 18 or later.

2. Morse-It: best for audio decoding and learning

Morse-It is the long-running favourite among radio amateurs, with over 1,000 ratings averaging 4.7. It decodes Morse audio through the microphone and calibrates speed, tone and volume automatically. It supports straight keys and iambic paddles and has a deep learning suite (Koch trainer, Farnsworth timing, CWops Academy method) as an $8.99 in-app purchase. It can flash messages with the flashlight but does not read lights with the camera.

3. Morse iCoder: a cheap all-rounder

Morse iCoder ($0.99, no in-app purchases) combines a flashlight sender, a live Morse key, a reference chart and a camera decoder. The developer labels the camera decoder experimental and gives you manual controls to adjust it for lighting and distance. It's a reasonable budget pick if you mainly send and only occasionally want to try decoding.

4. Morse Code: Offline Chat: best free option

A free app with no in-app purchases listed. It offers camera decoding with a manual brightness threshold and up to 10× zoom, flashlight or screen sending at 6–24 WPM, two-way “light chat” between phones, and practice games. It's newer (released 2026) and has too few ratings for the App Store to show an average yet.

Which should you pick?

  • Hiking, camping, boating or emergencies (reading another person's light): Morse Watcher.
  • Ham radio / CW audio: Morse-It.
  • Learning Morse from scratch: Morse-It's learning pack.
  • Free and casual: Morse Code: Offline Chat.
