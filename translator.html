How to use it

  1. Tap the dark Converted text box and type your message.
  2. The Morse code appears as you type. Tap Save.
  3. Watch the light flash your message in Morse.
  4. Tap the light to flash it full screen, for example to signal with a laptop or tablet screen.
  5. Change the ON and OFF times (in milliseconds) to slow the signal down or speed it up.
