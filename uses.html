Uses

When your phone has no signal

No bars, no Wi-Fi, no internet. Your flashlight and camera still work, and Morse Watcher turns them into a way to talk: one phone flashes a message, the other reads it as text. All you need is a clear line of sight to the light to communicate. Here's when it helps, plus a few times it's just handy.

Search & rescue SOS

Need rescue with no signal? Flash SOS, the international call for help, and make yourself easy to spot from a distance.

Hiking & camping

Talk across a valley, a ridge or between campsites with no signal.

Storms & blackouts

Reach neighbors across the street or from a rooftop when cell towers go down.

Night walks Alert

The free Alert strobe helps drivers see you better on dark roads and at crosswalks, for safer walks at night.

Internet shutdowns

Keep talking when networks are cut or throttled.

Learning Morse

Practice sending and reading, and check yourself against live decoding.

Scouts & games

Decode a blinking light in seconds at camp, in escape rooms or night games.

Try it free for 14 days

Works offline. SOS and Alert stay free.
